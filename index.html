About

Tyler Quattrin is a student at the University of Maryland’s Philip Merrill College of Journalism, pursuing a degree in journalism with minors in public policy and law and society.

He is a staff writer for The Diamondback, UMD’s independent student newspaper, where he has covered the College Park City Council and Student Government Association. A Twin Cities, Minnesota, native, Quattrin has also interned with the St. Paul Pioneer Press and Press Publications in White Bear Lake.

He has experience telling stories across multiple formats, including print and web writing, photojournalism, and natural-sound video. His reporting mostly includes coverage of local government, crime and courts, business and civil unrest. His photography has been featured in galleries in Minneapolis.
